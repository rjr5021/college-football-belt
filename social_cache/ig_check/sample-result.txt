The belt stays in South Bend. 🏆

Notre Dame 49, Purdue 10. Fourth defense of the Irish's 9th reign, now 301 days and counting since they took it from Stanford on Nov. 29.

This one was never in doubt. The Irish led 35–0 at halftime and coasted from there, piling up 444 total yards while holding the Boilermakers to 218. CJ Carr threw for 237 and 3 touchdowns (14 of 22, one pick), Aneyas Williams ran for 80 and a score, Micah Gilbert caught 2 for 83 including a 27-yard touchdown, and Bryce Young notched 2 sacks. Drayk Bowen returned a fumble 15 yards for a score in the second quarter.

Belt game No. 1,639 is in the books. Purdue's wait since 2018 continues.
